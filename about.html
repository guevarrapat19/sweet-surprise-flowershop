Our story

Built for thoughtful gifts

Sweet Surprise Flowershop believes the right flowers turn an ordinary day into a memory. We curate arrangements that feel personal—never rushed, never generic.

What we offer

From cheerful daisy rounds you can size to your budget, to signature bouquets like Sophie’s blend of tulips, lilies, fillers, and daisies, each piece is assembled with balance and care. Lily mesh wraps add a quick, affordable sparkle when you want something sweet without a big spend.

How shopping works

  • Browse featured arrangements on the shop page and open photos to see every angle.
  • Personalize where options appear—like stem count on select bouquets—before you add to cart.
  • Checkout with your contact details so we can coordinate delivery and special notes.

Shopping stays easy in the browser: your cart is saved on this device until you submit an order or clear it.
