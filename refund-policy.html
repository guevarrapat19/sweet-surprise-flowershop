Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled before preparation starts. Confirmed or dispatched orders may no longer be cancellable.

Refunds are considered for verified non-delivery, incorrect item, or quality concerns with supporting evidence.

Approved refund or replacement requests are processed after owner/admin review.
